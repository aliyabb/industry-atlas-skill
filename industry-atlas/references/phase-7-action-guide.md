# Phase 7 — Action guide

**Goal:** turn the atlas into a concrete plan for the user's purpose, grounded in their profile and in verified, dated facts (deadlines, prices, visas, events).

The folder is `13-How-to-Enter` (career), `13-Startup-Playbook` (founder), `13-Investor-Playbook` (investor) or `13-Action-Plan` (consultant, learning). Every guide links back into the atlas: segments, companies, opportunity map, content gaps.

## Rules for this phase

- Research every time-sensitive fact (deadlines, thresholds, prices, dates) and write "checked on YYYY-MM-DD".
- Tailor to the profile: name the user's strengths and gaps explicitly and build the plan around them.
- Surface urgent deadlines in the plan's first week and in your final message.
- Visa, legal, tax and investment content is informational, not professional advice.
- Personal trackers and contact lists stay outside the repository.

## Final message

Summarize all phases, the repo link, the three most important insights, urgent deadlines, and how to run the weekly routine.
